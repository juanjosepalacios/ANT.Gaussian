Au4-Au (5 atoms): one 4-atom layer and an apex atom, LSDA, half of a contact.

Each subdirectory is one variation of the calculation for this structure, with its own inputs,
outputs and README. The files keep the name of the system, `Au4-Au`.

- [spd-ghost/](spd-ghost/README.md): minimal spd basis and ECP, ghost electrodes on both sides. Not
  run.
