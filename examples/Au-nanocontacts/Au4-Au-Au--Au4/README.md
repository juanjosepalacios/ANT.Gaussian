Au4-Au-Au--Au4: the 10 atoms of [../Au4-Au-Au-Au4](../Au4-Au-Au-Au4/README.md) with a gap between
the apex atoms, between Bethe-lattice electrodes, LSDA.

Each subdirectory is one variation of the calculation for this structure, with its own inputs,
outputs and README. The files keep the name of the system, `Au4-Au-Au--Au4`.

- [s-FCC-s/](s-FCC-s/README.md): one s function on the 8 layer atoms, LANL2DZ on the apex atoms,
  1-orbital `FCC-s` electrodes.
