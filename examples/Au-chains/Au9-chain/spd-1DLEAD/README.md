`.ant`: `TYPE1 = TYPE2 = 1DLEAD`, `NPC = 1`, `NEMBED1 = NEMBED2 = 4`, `LEADDOS`, `ETA = 0.001`, `ALPHA = 0.05`, `SL = 0.001`.

## Outputs

`Au9-chain.log`, `T.Au9-chain.dat`, `Au9-chain.xyz`.

The stored outputs come from ANT 2.7.0 and were not rerun with ANT.Gaussian 3.0.0; they are kept for reference only.
The stored log terminates normally; T(E_F) = 1.992 e^2/h.

`P.Au9-chain.dat` is the density of the stored run. `Lead1DOS.dat`, `Lead1DOS.1.dat` and `Lead2DOS.dat` were loose in `examples/Au-chains`; they are lead DOS files written by `LEADDOS` and most likely belong to this run, the last one made there.
