Graphene ribbon of 304 atoms (C, with H on the edges), LSDA, between graphene Bethe-lattice
electrodes.

Each subdirectory is one variation of the calculation for this structure, with its own inputs,
outputs and README. The files keep the name of the system, `graphene-ribbon`.

- [sp-graphene/](sp-graphene/README.md): sp basis and ECP on C, 6-311G on H, `BLPAR = graphene`.
