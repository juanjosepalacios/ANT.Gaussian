Au chain Au6-chain (6 atoms) between one-dimensional leads, LSDA.

Each subdirectory is one variation of the calculation for this structure, with its own inputs,
outputs and README. The files keep the name of the system, `Au6-chain`.

- [spd-1DLEAD/](spd-1DLEAD/README.md): a minimal spd basis with a 68-electron ECP on all atoms, `TYPE1 = TYPE2 = 1DLEAD`.
