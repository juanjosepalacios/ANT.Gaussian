Au chain Au-Au-Au-Au (4 atoms) between one-dimensional leads, LSDA.

Each subdirectory is one variation of the calculation for this structure, with its own inputs,
outputs and README. The files keep the name of the system, `Au-Au-Au-Au`.

- [s-1DLEAD/](s-1DLEAD/README.md): one s function with a 68-electron ECP on all atoms, `TYPE1 = TYPE2 = 1DLEAD`.
