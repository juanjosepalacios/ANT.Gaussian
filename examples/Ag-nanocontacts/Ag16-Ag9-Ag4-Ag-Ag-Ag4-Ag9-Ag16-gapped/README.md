Ag tunnel junction Ag16-Ag9-Ag4-Ag-Ag-Ag4-Ag9-Ag16-gapped (60 atoms) between Bethe-lattice electrodes,
LSDA.

fcc stacking with a = 4.00 A, symmetric about z = 0: 16-atom layers at z = +-10, 9-atom layers at
+-8, 4-atom layers at +-6 and apex atoms on the axis at +-4 A, so the apex atoms are 8 A apart. The
charge is 0 and the multiplicity 1.

Each subdirectory is one variation of the calculation for this junction, with its own inputs, outputs
and README. The files keep the name of the system, `Ag16-Ag9-Ag4-Ag-Ag-Ag4-Ag9-Ag16-gapped`.

- [spd-Papacon-SOC-bias/](spd-Papacon-SOC-bias/README.md): spd basis and ECP on all atoms,
  Bethe-lattice electrodes `Papacon`, spin-orbit coupling enhanced 100 times and a bias of 1 V.
